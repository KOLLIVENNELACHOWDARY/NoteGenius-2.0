Physics Notes

1. Motion in a Straight Line

Motion is the change in position of an object with time. Speed is defined as the distance travelled per unit time. Velocity is speed in a given direction.

Equations of motion
For uniformly accelerated motion the following three equations hold:
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as
where v = final velocity, u = initial velocity, a = acceleration, t = time and s = displacement.

Newton's second law states that F = ma, where F is the net force and m is the mass.

Quantity    Symbol    SI Unit
Force    F    newton (N)
Mass    m    kilogram (kg)
Velocity    v    metre per second (m/s)

Important: Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

2. Work, Energy and Power

Work done W = F × d × cos θ. Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by a body due to its motion.
Formula: Kinetic energy: KE = 1/2 m v^2
Potential energy: PE = mgh

Types of energy:
- Kinetic energy
- Potential energy
- Thermal energy

| Unit | Quantity | Formula |
|------|----------|---------|
| Joule | Energy | F × d |
| Watt | Power | W / t |
