# Mathematics Revision

## Algebra
A quadratic equation is an equation of the form ax^2 + bx + c = 0.
The solutions are given by the quadratic formula:
x = (-b ± sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / (2a)
Discriminant: D = b^2 - 4ac
The arithmetic progression formula: a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d
Sum of n terms: S_n = n/2 (2a + (n-1)d)
Identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

## Calculus
The derivative of x^n is n x^(n-1). The integral ∫ x^n dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C.
$$\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{3}$$
Area of a circle = πr²
Circumference = 2πr
Pythagoras theorem states that a² + b² = c².
